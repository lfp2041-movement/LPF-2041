Community Engagement

Coordinator's Field Guide

Is LPF an official constitutional civic movement?

Yes. LPF operates strictly under Article 72 of the Constitution of the Republic of Uganda, exercising citizens' fundamental right to organize and advocate for civic transformation through peaceful avenues.

Does LPF distribute political cash handouts?

No. Handouts buy temporary silence, but systems create lasting wealth. LPF builds the 83% Revenue Shield so your district keeps its wealth to build permanent roads, schools, and hospitals.
